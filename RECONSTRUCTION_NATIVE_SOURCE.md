# KirthiVerse Native Reconstruction Contract

## Authority
Recovered Manus V4 Phase-3 source is the next-gen visual/interaction candidate, reconciled against V3.1 production truth and canonical content. The native source includes AnimatedTeacher, InteractiveWhiteboard, HiTechConsole, Worlds, World Detail, Lessons, Search, Progress, Profile and role dashboards, plus its own PWA surface.

## No-donor UI rule
Do not use ArivuKids, DivyaNexus, Kandan, RamaVerse, SakthiAI or OmSaravanaBhava as visual/layout donors. Donor material is limited to verified missing content, rollback or provenance evidence.

## Native design target
A distinctive learning universe rather than a generic dashboard: animated teachers, interactive classroom/lab surfaces, world-based discovery, age-adaptive learning, progress and role-aware experiences. Preserve the native Editorial Garden Classroom / learning-world identity where present; do not force devotional or enterprise-AI styling.

## Data truth
V3.1 remains the verified production candidate authority for release truth; V4 Phase-3 is the future isolated visual/interaction candidate. Canonical export evidence records 135 lessons, 72 assessments and 11 worlds.

## Required gates before main
- V4 clean build and regression comparison against V3.1 authority.
- No canonical lesson/assessment/world loss.
- AnimatedTeacher, Whiteboard, HiTechConsole and core learning flows functional.
- Desktop/tablet/mobile QA.
- PWA/offline/update recovery verified.
- Accessibility, keyboard and reduced-motion verified.
- No cross-site duplication of hero, nav, card grammar, background treatment or motion grammar.
- Existing production remains rollback-safe until exact candidate is proven.

## Release rule
Do not promote the older ArivuKids-derived shell merely because CI is green. Native-source fidelity and visual differentiation are mandatory release gates.
