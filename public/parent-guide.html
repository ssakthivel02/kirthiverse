For parents and guardians

Support progress without pressure.

KirthiVerse provides lessons, quizzes, local progress evidence, family goals, mistake review, study planning and wellbeing controls. In the current release, learner information stays in the browser being used.

Start safely

  1. Create a learner nickname and avatar. A full legal name is not required.
  2. Select an age band, learning level and optional favourite subjects.
  3. Choose a realistic daily or weekly goal.
  4. Export the local record before clearing browser data or changing devices.

Useful family tools

  • Parent View: lesson, quiz, XP and mastery evidence.
  • Family Goals: weekly targets and healthy session limits.
  • Study Planner: a seven-day plan based on current needs.
  • Mistake Review: incorrect answers with explanations and retry links.
  • Weekly Review: printable activity reflection.
  • Wellbeing Centre: optional breaks and session timing without penalties.

Information not required

Do not enter passwords, home addresses, phone numbers, precise location, precise birth details, Aadhaar, APAAR, photographs, medical information or other sensitive personal details into learner profiles or learning notes.

Current limitation: this release does not provide remote parent monitoring, school rosters, teacher accounts or cross-device score synchronisation.

Review and reset

Use Settings and Data to export, import or reset the complete local record. Read the learner privacy notice, retention explanation and support guidance.
