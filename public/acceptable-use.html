Safe learning rules

Use KirthiVerse to learn, practise and grow.

These rules apply to learners, parents, teachers, tutors and school users. They support a safe, respectful and reliable learning environment.

Do

  • Use a nickname instead of unnecessary personal information.
  • Complete lessons and quizzes honestly.
  • Take breaks and stop when tired.
  • Ask a trusted adult or teacher when content is unclear.
  • Report broken content, unsafe behaviour or security concerns.
  • Use exported learning records responsibly and keep them private.

Do not

  • Enter passwords, phone numbers, home addresses, precise location, Aadhaar, APAAR, medical information or private family details into learning fields.
  • Attempt to access another learner, family, teacher or school record.
  • Share another person’s exported progress file without permission.
  • Manipulate scores, XP, streaks or assignments.
  • Upload harmful, unlawful, abusive or copyrighted material without permission.
  • Use KirthiVerse to bully, threaten, discriminate or impersonate others.

Current local-first release

The current website does not provide open messaging, public child profiles, public learner search or a public leaderboard. School and account capabilities must not be represented as active until the secure identity and school releases are deployed and approved.

Concerns

Use the support and grievance guidance for product, privacy or safety concerns. Use the instructions in security.txt for security vulnerabilities.
