Privacy for learners

Your learning belongs to you.

KirthiVerse helps you read lessons, practise quizzes and see your progress. This page explains what happens to your information in the current website release.

What is saved?

Your nickname, avatar, age group, favourite subjects, completed lessons, quiz scores, bookmarks, goals and settings may be saved in this browser.

Where is it saved?

It stays on this device and in this browser. It is not automatically sent to a school, teacher, parent account, advertiser or public leaderboard.

What should you never share?

Do not type your home address, phone number, personal email, exact birthday, Aadhaar number, APAAR number, password or private photograph into lesson or quiz fields.

Can you remove it?

Yes. An adult can export or clear the local learner record from Settings and Data. Clearing browser data may also remove it.

Need help?

Ask a parent, guardian or teacher you trust. You can also read the full privacy notice or visit the Help Centre.
