Accessibility at KirthiVerse

KirthiVerse aims to provide a clear and usable learning experience for children, families and teachers across keyboard, touch and assistive-technology use.

Controls available now

  • Keyboard-visible focus states and a skip-to-content link.
  • Reduced-motion and larger-text preferences in Settings.
  • Responsive layouts designed from 320px mobile widths upward.
  • Page titles, route announcements and labelled controls.
  • Text alternatives or accessible labels for functional icons.
  • Print-friendly lesson and progress-report layouts.

Current target

The engineering target is WCAG 2.2 AA. This statement is not a claim that an independent accessibility audit has been completed. Formal browser, screen-reader and expert review remains a production gate.

Known limitations

  • Some legacy static learning pages may not match the newest application styling.
  • Complex charts use accompanying tables or text summaries, but further assistive-technology review is required.
  • Offline and install behaviour depends on browser capabilities.

Report a barrier

Describe the page, device, browser and task that was difficult. Do not include a child password or unnecessary personal information. Visit Support and grievance information.
